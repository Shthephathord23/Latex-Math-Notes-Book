\section{Reduction: Gaussian Elimination and Normal Forms}
\label{sec:rank-normal-form}

\begin{remark}[Roadmap --- section under construction]
    This section completes the basis-free analysis begun in
    Section~\ref{sec:equivalence-similarity}. Two maps are equivalent exactly when they share a
    rank, and a suitable pair of bases reduces any $T : U \to V$ to a \emph{partial identity} of
    size $\Rank{T}$ --- the \textbf{rank normal form}, the complete invariant of equivalence
    (foretold in Remark~\ref{rem:basis-free-content}). In finite dimensions the form is
    \emph{computed} by \textbf{Gaussian elimination}, under the dictionary
    \[
        \text{row operations} \;=\; \text{codomain (left) change of basis}, \qquad
        \text{column operations} \;=\; \text{domain (right) change of basis},
    \]
    and matrix inversion is the special case of solving $A v_j = \CanonicalVector{j}$ for the unknown
    columns $v_j$ of $A^{-1}$, one block-column at a time. The injectivity, surjectivity, and bijectivity
    normal forms ($\begin{psmallmatrix} I \\ 0 \end{psmallmatrix}$,
    $\begin{psmallmatrix} I & 0 \end{psmallmatrix}$, $I$) are the extreme cases. The full
    development, together with triangular/flag form, is deferred to the next implementation pass;
    the porting checklist is recorded in the source of this file.
\end{remark}
